INSTALL TradeNexa

Follow this 4-step walk-through to add TradeNexa to your TradingView charts.

01

Verify TradingView Username

Ensure your TradingView username is correctly linked in your Account settings. TradeNexa is delivered via TradingView's proprietary Invite-Only permission mechanism.

02

Log into TradingView Chart

Open TradingView in your web browser or desktop app at tradingview.com and load any chart (e.g. BTCUSD or EURUSD).

03

Open "Invite-Only Scripts" Section

Click the Indicators & Strategies button (fx icon) at the top of your chart. In the left panel of the modal, select Invite-Only Scripts. Locate TradeNexa Algorithmic Engine and click it once.

04

Configure Parameters & Alerts

Double-click the TradeNexa indicator title on your chart to customize sensitivity, toggle BUY/SELL markers, or configure TradingView mobile alerts with three clicks.
