Refund Policy

We strive to ensure every TradeNexa member has a fair and transparent experience. Please read our refund policy carefully before purchasing.

Last Updated: October 2026
⚠ Legal Review Notice This refund policy is provided as a reasonable placeholder template. It should be reviewed and approved by qualified legal counsel before deployment. TradeNexa makes no representation that this policy satisfies all applicable consumer protection laws in every jurisdiction.
01 — Overview

Overview of Refund Policy

At TradeNexa, we value transparency and fairness. This Refund Policy outlines the conditions under which refunds may be issued for purchases of our trading indicator subscription plans available on the TradingView platform.

By purchasing any TradeNexa plan — Monthly Subscription or Lifetime Access — you agree to the terms stated in this policy. We encourage you to review this document carefully before making a purchase decision.

Refund eligibility depends on the plan type, the timing of the request, and whether the circumstances fall within our eligible refund criteria outlined below.

02 — Monthly Plans

Monthly Subscription Refunds

Monthly subscribers may be eligible for a refund under the following conditions:

  • The refund request is submitted within 7 days of the initial purchase or renewal date.
  • The subscriber has not extensively used the indicator signals during the eligible refund period (i.e., the subscription has not been actively used for more than 3 trading sessions).
  • The request is made in good faith and is the first refund request associated with that account.

Renewal Note: Refunds are generally not provided for auto-renewal charges if adequate cancellation notice was not given prior to the renewal date. You may cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings to prevent future renewals.

Approved monthly refunds will be processed to the original payment method within the processing time described in Section 6.

03 — Lifetime Plan

Lifetime Plan Refunds

Due to the nature of one-time lifetime access, refund eligibility for the Lifetime Plan is more limited:

  • Refund requests must be submitted within 14 days of the original purchase date.
  • The account must not have made extensive use of the TradeNexa indicator beyond initial evaluation (generally defined as fewer than 5 trading sessions of active use).
  • Technical issues that rendered the product entirely non-functional — where our support team was unable to resolve the issue within a reasonable timeframe — may qualify for a full refund regardless of the above conditions.

Important: Lifetime plan refunds are evaluated on a case-by-case basis. Submitting a refund request does not guarantee approval. Our support team will assess each request individually and respond within the timeframe outlined in Section 6.

04 — Exceptions

Exceptions and Non-Refundable Situations

Refunds will not be issued in the following circumstances:

  • The refund request is submitted outside the eligible time window for the respective plan.
  • The account has been suspended or terminated due to a violation of our Terms of Service.
  • The refund request is based solely on trading losses or dissatisfaction with market outcomes. TradeNexa is a decision-support tool and does not guarantee profitable trades.
  • The purchaser claims they were unaware of the product's nature, after having accessed and used the indicator on TradingView.
  • The request is made for a plan purchased through a third-party promotional partner or marketplace (such requests should be directed to the respective platform).
  • The refund eligibility window has passed, regardless of circumstances.

Trading Losses Are Not Grounds for Refund: TradeNexa provides technical analysis indicators as decision-support tools only. Financial losses incurred from trading decisions made using our indicators do not constitute grounds for a refund. Please review our Risk Disclaimer before using our products.

05 — How to Request

How to Request a Refund

Refund requests should be submitted to support@tradenexa.com within the eligible period.

To ensure your request is processed efficiently, please include the following in your email:

  1. 1Your registered email address associated with your TradeNexa account.
  2. 2The date of your purchase and the plan type (Monthly or Lifetime).
  3. 3Your order or transaction ID (available in your confirmation email or billing section).
  4. 4A clear description of the reason for your refund request.
  5. 5Any relevant screenshots or supporting documentation (especially for technical issue claims).

Incomplete requests may delay processing. Our team will acknowledge your request within 2 business days.

06 — Processing Time

Processing Time

Once a refund request is approved, processing times are as follows:

  • Review Decision: Within 3–5 business days of receiving a complete refund request.
  • Payment Reversal: Once approved, refunds are typically reflected within 5–10 business days, depending on your bank or payment processor.
  • Notification: You will receive an email confirmation once your refund has been approved and initiated.

Currency Note: Refunds are issued in the original currency of the transaction. TradeNexa is not responsible for any exchange rate differences or bank fees that may apply to international refunds.

07 — Contact

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Refund Policy or need assistance with a refund request, please contact our support team:

Support Email: support@tradenexa.com
Response Time: Within 2 business days
Support Hours: Monday – Friday, 10:00 AM – 6:00 PM IST

We are committed to resolving all support queries promptly and fairly. For general questions, you may also visit our FAQ page.
