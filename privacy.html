1. Overview

TradeNexa ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This policy outlines how we collect, handle, and protect your information when utilizing our website and TradingView script licensing infrastructure.

We only collect information strictly required to provision and maintain your TradingView software license:

Your details are processed to grant access to indicator scripts, issue software updates, enforce licensing keys, and communicate critical technical changes.

4. Third-Party Disclosures

We do not rent, sell, or trade your data. Information is shared only with trusted infrastructure providers (TradingView API, Cloudflare, secure payment gateways) necessary to deliver your purchased service.

5. Security

All communication is secured via modern TLS 1.3 encryption. Passwords and license tokens are stored using salted cryptographic hashes.
