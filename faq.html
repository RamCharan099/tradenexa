FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Everything you need to know about TradeNexa licensing, TradingView integration, and signals.

What is TradeNexa? +
TradeNexa is an institutional-grade technical indicator designed exclusively for the TradingView platform. It simplifies complex market structure into high-probability visual BUY and SELL signals to support systematic trading decisions.
Do I need a paid TradingView subscription? +
No, TradeNexa functions seamlessly on the 100% Free plan of TradingView. You do not require a Pro or Premium TradingView tier to run the indicator on your charts.
Which markets and timeframes can I trade? +
TradeNexa works across all assets available on TradingView, including Cryptocurrencies (BTC, ETH, SOL), Forex (EURUSD, GBPUSD, JPY pairs), Commodities (Gold, Silver, Crude Oil), and Major Indices (S&P500, Nasdaq). It supports all standard timeframes from 1-minute scalp charts to 1-day swing charts.
Do TradeNexa signals repaint? +
No. TradeNexa signals lock permanently once the respective timeframe candle closes. This ensures complete algorithmic transparency for both live execution and historical backtesting.
How do I receive access after purchasing? +
Immediately after checkout, you enter your TradingView username in your TradeNexa Dashboard. Our automated provisioning engine grants your TradingView account Invite-Only script permission within minutes.
Does TradeNexa guarantee profitable returns? +
No indicator can guarantee profits. TradeNexa is a technical decision-support analysis tool. Sustainable trading requires strict personal risk management and disciplined strategy execution.
What is the difference between Monthly and Lifetime? +
Monthly is a flexible recurring subscription that you can cancel anytime. Lifetime is a single one-time payment providing permanent access to the indicator and all future algorithmic updates with no recurring fees.
